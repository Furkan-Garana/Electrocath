What It Is

What is cathodic protection?

Metallic pipelines and structures buried in soil or submerged in water are exposed to corrosion over time. Cathodic protection is an electrochemical technique used to control that corrosion, helping extend the working life of the infrastructure it protects.

Corrosion isn't just a maintenance nuisance — left unmanaged, it can compromise the structural integrity of pipelines carrying gas, water, or other critical utilities. Addressing it early is far more practical than repairing damage after the fact.

The Concept

How the protection works

Introduced gradually so it's understandable without an engineering background.

⚡

Current is applied

A protective electrical current is directed at the pipeline surface.

🔗

Corrosion is redirected

The corrosion reaction is shifted away from the protected structure.

📈

Life is extended

The pipeline's working life is extended under continued monitoring.
