HT / LT Distribution

Electrical distribution infrastructure, installed with care.

HT and LT distribution networks are made up of lines, supporting structures, electrical equipment and transformers working together to deliver power safely and reliably.

Our service presentation covers erection and installation work associated with HT/LT lines and transformers, followed by the necessary testing and commissioning activities for project handover.

Typical project scope varies by site and contract. The final equipment, quantities and testing requirements are determined from the approved project specification.

HT Line Erection

LT Line Erection

Transformer erection and associated installation work as required by the project scope.
