What It Is

Turning your roof into a power source

Rooftop solar uses panels mounted on your roof to convert sunlight into electricity for your home, reducing how much power you draw from the grid. A correctly sized, correctly installed system is a long-term piece of home infrastructure — which is why we treat every step, from assessment to commissioning, seriously.
